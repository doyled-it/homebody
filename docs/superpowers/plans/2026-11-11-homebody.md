# homebody Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Comfort 72°F when anyone is home and 82°F after both phones have been gone 20 minutes, decided from the house's own network and applied to the Nest over Matter, with a Climate screen in the room picker.

**Architecture:** A new `homebody` repo (Python, uv) runs on the hub as two services: a Matter controller and the presence-and-decision daemon. snaphouse installs and self-updates it (a `homebody_bootstrap` role), serves its status file and API through nginx, and shows a Climate screen.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3 with uv, `python-matter-server` (Matter controller), stdlib networking (neighbour table, ARP probe), BlueZ `l2ping`, pytest and ruff; Ansible and nginx in snaphouse; ES modules with `node --test` for the page.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-homebody-design.md` (homebody). Its decisions, thresholds, and status shape are binding.

## Global Constraints

- No em dashes anywhere. No identifying data in either repo or in requests: no MAC or IP addresses, names, or pairing codes in tracked files; they live only in `/etc/homebody/local.toml` (shape in `local.example.toml`).
- uv for Python everywhere; homebody's runtime runs from its own uv-synced venv.
- TDD; full suites before each commit; snaphouse CI's container-apply and art-share idempotency gates stay green; never chown the shared public root; every new nginx route has a test.
- Values: comfort 72°F, away 82°F, away delay 20 min, Wi-Fi probe every 60 s, Bluetooth fallback only after 5 min unseen on Wi-Fi and at most once a minute, thermostat commands at most once a minute. Matter cooling setpoints are in hundredths of °C (72°F = 2222, 82°F = 2778).
- Each task is its own branch and PR; CI green before merge.

## Review Focus

1. A phone that sleeps its Wi-Fi for 10 to 15 minutes while someone is home: the house stays occupied (20-minute window plus Bluetooth fallback).
2. The Matter controller or the thermostat unreachable: homebody keeps tracking presence, retries with backoff, never crashes, and acts on the latest state once it reconnects (not a backlog of transitions).
3. A manual change made on the Nest: honoured until the next occupied/unoccupied transition, then automation resumes.
4. A disabled person (the partner until they agree) never affects occupancy and is never probed.
5. A hub reboot: presence starts unknown, the thermostat is not changed until presence has been observed for one full away window or someone is seen (no false "away" right after boot).
